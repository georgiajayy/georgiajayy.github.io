What does the data suggest about a third straight title at Euro 2029 in Germany?

W H A T   T H E   D A T A   S A Y S
  • England grew into the tournament, then survivedOut-created by France in the opener, dominant against the Netherlands and Wales, then out-created again in the final, and still won on penalties.
  • Volume, not qualitySpain took 23 shots to England's 8, but shot for shot England's chances were just as good.
  • Penalties distorted the raw numbersWith penalties removed, Russo and Toone led England's open-play threat.
  • Spain are risingEngland win through clinical finishing rather than dominance, and have allowed more chances at every tournament since 2019.
  • England's attack is heading towards 309 of 17 attacking contributors will be 30+ by Euro 2029, accounting for 49.8% of attacking involvement.
L O O K i N G   B A C K

The most valuable moments came from the data correcting me. My first read of the final's shot map was that Spain had better chances; the numbers showed they simply had more of them. Checking visuals against numbers is now a habit.

The analysis has honest limits: four tournaments is a short trend, some samples are small, and it describes what happened rather than predicting what will. Next, I'd like to use WSL data to find young English players performing well at club level, and build my own expected goals model.
