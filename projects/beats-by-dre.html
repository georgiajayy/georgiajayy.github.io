01 BRAND, STRATEGY & CULTURAL RESEARCH

How Do You Sell Sound Through Culture?

Beats by Dre / Creative Strategy Case Study

Beats has built a recognisable identity through music, sport and cultural influence. The strategic question is how that attention can translate into credibility, product relevance and consumer consideration.

01 / THE QUESTION

Can cultural attention become consumer connection?

I analysed 25 headphone advertisements, 10 from Beats by Dre and 15 from competitors, including Apple, Sony, Bose and JBL. I examined talent, hooks, platforms, production styles, duration and creative execution.

I then tested four advertisements with consumers to explore which approaches attracted attention, felt believable and encouraged purchase consideration. The aim was to identify how Beats could retain its cultural influence while making its product story more persuasive.

02 / WHAT THE RESEARCH FOUND

Attention and trust are not the same thing.

50%OF BEATS ADS IN THE SAMPLE FEATURED CELEBRITY TALENT
13.3%OF COMPETITOR ADS IN THE SAMPLE FEATURED CELEBRITY TALENT
8 / 21RESPONDENTS SELECTED THE BEATS CELEBRITY AD AS MOST ATTENTION-GRABBING

  1. Celebrity talent attracts attention, but does not automatically build trust.

    Five of the ten Beats advertisements featured celebrity talent, compared with two of the 15 competitor advertisements. In the consumer test, the Beats Celebrity advert received the most attention, with eight of 21 respondents selecting it.

    However, only three of 21 considered it the most believable. Its average believability rating was 3.0, the lowest of the four adverts tested. Recognition created visibility, but did not automatically make the product story convincing.

    EVIDENCE / PROJECT 01: COMPETITIVE AD ANALYSIS · PROJECT 02: CONSUMER FEEDBACK

  2. Experience-led sport creative made a stronger product connection.

    The Beats Athlete / Sport advert was the one respondents found most believable and most likely to make them want to buy. This contrasted with the Celebrity advert, which attracted the most attention.

    It suggests audiences may respond more positively when talent appears in a context where the product has a clear role. Sport and performance can make product benefits feel more tangible and credible.

    EVIDENCE / PROJECT 02: 21 VALID CONSUMER RESPONSES

  3. Creator-led formats offer a direction worth testing.

    Two of the three Bose advertisements in the sample featured creator or influencer talent, and two used low-production, UGC-style content. This is a useful competitive observation and a prompt to explore more platform-native creative, not proof that creator-led advertising performs better.

03 / THE STRATEGIC READ

Keep the cultural currency. Strengthen the product story.

THE CENTRAL CREATIVE PRINCIPLE

Culture should attract attention, not replace the product story.

The research points towards a balance, not a wholesale change in direction. Beats can continue to use influential talent, music and cultural references, while ensuring the product has a meaningful role in the creative. Authenticity should be designed into the execution, and platform relevance should not come at the expense of brand identity.

04 / RECOMMENDATIONS

Three ways to make influence more persuasive.

01 / EXPERIENCE-LED

Make the product part of the experience.

Build campaigns around artists, athletes and cultural figures in situations where the headphones have a meaningful role. Show how the product fits into a real routine, performance or moment, rather than relying on recognition alone.

02 / CREATOR-LED

Introduce a creator strand alongside major talent.

Experiment with relevant creators and emerging cultural voices through more relatable, platform-native storytelling. This can complement Beats' established celebrity-led campaigns, not replace them.

03 / CREATIVE TESTING

Test the opening, not just the finished advert.

Compare curiosity-driven questions, unexpected visual openings and experience-led hooks against traditional celebrity introductions. Assess which approach sustains attention and strengthens the product story.

THE NEXT TEST / CELEBRITY-LED VS EXPERIENCE-LED

VERSION A / CULTURAL INFLUENCE

Celebrity-led creative focused on recognition and cultural relevance.

VERSION B / PRODUCT EXPERIENCE

An athlete or relevant creator using the product in a real-world context.

Keep the product and intended audience consistent. Compare initial attention, brand recall, perceived authenticity, believability and purchase consideration. The objective is to find the strongest balance between attention and persuasion, not to judge success on views alone.

06 / THE CREATIVE SYSTEM

Turn the insight into repeatable standards.

The accompanying Creative Strategy Framework translates the research into a practical system for developing and evaluating future Beats advertising. It includes a platform playbook, brand creative standards, an evaluation rubric and a strategic recommendations summary.

Its platform playbook sets out three universal rules, alongside two rules each for YouTube, TikTok and Meta. The wider framework applies those principles to Beats' voice, look, sound and talent, then proposes a five-criterion creative evaluation rubric.

THE CREATIVE STANDARD

Cultural relevance should attract attention. Authentic storytelling should build trust. Product relevance should encourage consideration.

The creative evaluation rubric is an independent framework, not an industry-standard accreditation.
