Pick your poison

Welcome to the crypt, where I keep the little cursed games I build when the spreadsheets go quiet. Some are about data, some are about football, and some are just about hitting things that scream.

Everything here is made by me, usually at 2am, usually with a horror film on in the background. Expect bugs. Expect bar charts with teeth. Expect to lose.

Each game below is a grave. The open ones you can play right now. The sealed ones are still being dug up, so check back soon and see what crawled out.

Found a bug, beat a high score, or want to build something together? Email me at work.georgiajayy@gmail.com.
