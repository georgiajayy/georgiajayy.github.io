About me

Hi, I’m Georgia

I’m a data analyst and creative from Birmingham who turns audience insight into stories people actually care about. I studied film at UCL, spent years behind the camera making football content, then learned to make the numbers talk too.

I’m happiest where sport, culture and data collide: working out what makes fans tick, why something takes off online, and how to tell that story so it lands. I’ve made content and campaigns with Arsenal, BBC Sport, ITV Sport, The FA, Nike, Adidas, EA Sports and UEFA, and I coach grassroots football at weekends.

What I do

  • Data & insight

    Excel, SQL, Python and Power BI. Cleaning messy data, building dashboards and turning trends into clear recommendations.

  • Content & social

    Social strategy, campaign planning, short-form video, photography and editing, with analytics guiding every post.

  • Coaching & community

    FA Level 1 coach. Mentoring, safeguarding and helping young players build confidence on and off the pitch.
