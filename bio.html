I.

THE CD YEARS

Our tale begins with a ten-year-old and a CD collection that got completely out of hand. While other kids collected stickers, young G was building towers of albums, reading every sleeve note, and kicking a football against any wall that would take it.

Music, film, sport: she wanted all of it, and she wanted it loud.

II.

BEHIND THE CAMERA

Years later, deep in a Media (Film Production) degree at UCL, news arrived on the wind: COPA90 needed a production intern. It was summer 2023.

She fell upon the opportunity like a punk on a battered guitar.

She dug up stories for football content that went on to hit 1M+ views across TikTok and Instagram, and got stuck into branded shoots for Nike, Adidas, EA Sports and UEFA.

In June 2024 she graduated with a 2:1, threw her cap in the air, and walked straight back onto the pitch.

III.

FOR THE GIRLS

What followed was a full tour of the beautiful game. Grassroots campaigns at Bloomsbury Football Foundation. Growing a football charity's following by 2,000+ in under three months at League Leaders.

Then the big one: the Google Pixel Powerhouse Project, researching and shaping campaigns with Arsenal, BBC Sport, ITV Sport and The FA to

push the women's game further than ever.

Along the way she dug into culture for the content team at 411, and today she's the digital media specialist at BLAQ UK (UK Black Pride).
